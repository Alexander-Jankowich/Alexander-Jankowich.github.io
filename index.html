$P+ Gesture Recognizer

A standalone C++ multistroke gesture recognizer, ported from a game-engine module and compiled to WebAssembly. Try it in your browser · Source

This was originally a major feature of a game developed over the spring semester of my junior year in a group of 9. I was particularly proud of how the recognizer turned out so I chose to port it out of the CUGL game engine.
